# NovrPulse
## Direction
An expressive digital newspaper for readers following cybersecurity in Nigeria, across Africa and around the world. A broad serif masthead and a single photograph carry the opening spread. A ruled news index carries the eye to smaller stories. Readers can move from region to source and related coverage, then subscribe to the briefing.
## Editorial references
The Guardian's security archive groups dated stories under a persistent topic heading. Pulse retains a dateline beside each headline and gives each region a permanent URL. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/data-computer-security)

WIRED's Security page gives the opening story an image and a standfirst, then changes density for narrower subject lists. Pulse uses that change of pace between the lead, the rail and the archive. [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/category/security/)

AP places topic links, top stories and newsletter choices together. Pulse keeps region navigation in the masthead and subscription at the end of every reading path. [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/)
## Type and palette
Georgia provides a readily available editorial face with real italics. Inter carries controls, sources and dates. Display size scales fluidly. Article copy sits within 65 characters per line. The purple brand ink is #632D8A, the blue is #4353A3 and the signal red is #E3192A. The publication uses warm paper in light mode. The staff portal uses a cool gray canvas, white fields and panels, and slate text. Its dark theme uses charcoal surfaces with lighter blue and purple accents. Backend presentation values have a finite vocabulary.
## Staff controls

Purple identifies primary actions and selected filters. Cobalt identifies secondary actions, search icons and keyboard focus. Red marks destructive actions, invalid fields and the editorial heading rule. Controls use shared surface, border and foreground tokens for rest, hover, press, focus and disabled states. Selected filters expose their state through `aria-pressed`. Inputs keep a solid fill and a visible boundary on both the canvas and a panel.

## Layout and media
A wide opening spread gives the lead roughly two thirds of the width. At tablet width the rail moves below the lead. On a phone the photograph, headline and source form one reading sequence. Newsroom controls have soft corners and full labels. The public pages retain squared images, thin rules and generous headline spacing. Generic edition photography is captioned independently of news events. Story-specific photographs carry alternative text and a credit.
