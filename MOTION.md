# NovrPulse motion
The experience is expressive in scale and restrained in movement. The lead enters over 220 milliseconds. The region navigation stays available as the masthead scrolls away. Pointer and keyboard interactions reveal an underline and a small directional movement on reading links. Native scrolling preserves touch and browser history. Reduced motion removes transforms and transitions. The reading order, menu and newsletter remain usable without motion.

The newsroom kingfisher tilts its head when a section title is hovered. Its flight pose opens and settles its paper wing once when readers approach the newsletter or focus the form. Both poses stay still with reduced motion.

The tab kingfisher lifts its folded newspaper wing twice and opens its bill twice over about two seconds. Its first gesture starts after six seconds in a visible tab. Subsequent gestures follow 24 seconds of rest. Hidden tabs and reduced-motion preferences keep the resting bird. The PNG poses, touch icon and ICO are rendered from the tab artwork with `node frontend/scripts/render-kingfisher.mjs`.
